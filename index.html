    Tesla Powerwall 3 specs used in this demo

    Powerwall 3

    • 13.5 kWh nominal energy
    • 11.5 kW continuous AC power
    • 15.4 kW off-grid continuous (11.5 kW config, 80 A breaker)
    • 5 kW charge without expansions
    • Up to 20 kW DC solar input
    • 185 LRA motor-start capability
    • Up to 4 units per system

    Expansion module

    • 13.5 kWh nominal energy
    • No inverter — does not add kW
    • 8 kW charge on the leader Powerwall 3 when expansions are installed
    • Up to 3 expansions per system
    • Must connect to the leader Powerwall 3
    • Maximum system: 4 Powerwall 3 + 3 expansions = 94.5 kWh, 46 kW

    Source: Tesla Powerwall 3 Datasheet (US, 2025). Runtime assumes a full battery at the start of an outage and a steady load equal to annual kWh ÷ 8,760 hours. Real outages vary with weather, heat pumps, well pumps, and how much of the house is backed up.
